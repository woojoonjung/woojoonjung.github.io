Biography.

I am a graduate student at the DAIS Lab, Korea University, advised by Prof. Susik Yoon. My primary research focus is Representation Learning and Human–AI Interaction, centered on building systems that enable effective human–AI collaboration. With a background in both industrial design and computer science, I strive to bridge technical rigor with human-centered design.
